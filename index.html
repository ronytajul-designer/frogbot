Questions founders ask first.

Can your team build and migrate the app for us?

Yes. That is FrogBot Enterprise. Our engineers connect to your current tools, deliver a working prototype on your real data in 48 hours, and ship the production app in 2 weeks for a fixed price. You own the code and the data.

Do I ever pay per seat, editor or end user?

Never. Whether 5 or 50,000 people sign in to your app on Dojo, you pay per app.

Can I get a surprise usage bill?

No. Compute, database and bandwidth are inside the $29 flat price. Nothing raises your bill without your click.

What happens if I cancel Dojo?

You keep your code and your data. Every FrogBot app is a standard MIT-licensed TypeScript repository. Clone it and export your database at any time.

Why can I not type my own request at the top of this page?

The bar at the top is a demonstration. FrogBot does not build inside a browser sandbox. Your coding agent, such as Claude Code, Cursor or Codex, builds with FrogBot on your own machine. Or our engineers build it for you.

How is FrogBot different from Lovable, Bolt or Retool?

Code generators leave you with a large codebase to maintain. Hosted builders keep the app on their platform and charge per user. FrogBot is an open-source framework: a short config file, and an app you own outright.
